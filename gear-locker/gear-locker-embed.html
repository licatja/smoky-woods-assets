What it is

Company gifts people actually want, without the spreadsheet

Instead of guessing sizes, colors and who wants what, you choose a short lineup and a budget. We set up a private Gear Locker™ page with your company's name on it. Everyone picks for themselves before the close date, and we engrave the whole batch at once.

For your people

A simple page with your logo. They pick a gift and a color, add their name, and send. No account, no prices, no hassle.

For you

One quote up front, one list of every pick, and one invoice at the end. No chasing replies or tallying emails.

Made by hand

Every item is laser engraved in our East Tennessee shop by Smoky Woods, a veteran-owned small business.

How it works

Four steps from idea to delivered

  1. Pick the lineup

    Choose the items, the budget per person and how many each person picks. We send a quote and a digital proof of your logo.

  2. Your Gear Locker™ opens

    Approve the proof and pay the 50% deposit. You get a private link to share with your team.

  3. Your people choose

    Everyone picks their gift and color by the close date. They can change their mind until then.

  4. We engrave and ship

    You get the full list of who picked what. We engrave, pack and ship, then send one final invoice.

Questions

Good to know

Do my employees see prices?

No. Your Gear Locker™ shows only the gifts and their choices. Prices stay between you and us.

Does anyone need to make an account?

No. Your people open the link, pick, and send. That's it.

Whose name is on the page?

Yours. Each Gear Locker™ page carries your company's name and logo. A small "Powered by Gear Locker™" line at the bottom is the only mention of us.

When do we pay?

A 50% deposit when your Gear Locker™ opens, and the balance on one invoice once everything is made and shipped.

What if someone misses the close date?

Tell us before engraving starts and we can usually add late picks. You'll see the list of who has picked before anything is made.

Ready to open a Gear Locker™ for your team?

Build a lineup in a few minutes and send it to us. We'll reply with a real quote and a digital proof. Nothing is made until you approve it.
