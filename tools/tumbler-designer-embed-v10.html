Pick a tumbler, add your art, and see exactly where the laser takes the paint off. Measure twice, engrave once.

How it works
  1. Pick your tumbler. Size, handle, color and the metal under the coat, in the Tumbler box.
  2. Pick where it's engraved. Front, front and back, or all the way around.
  3. Add art and words. Upload a picture, choose one of our designs, or tap Add text. Everything you add is listed under Your design. Click an item there to change it.
  4. Arrange it on the Layout. That's the tumbler unrolled flat. Drag to move, pull the corner square to resize, and drag the round dot to rotate. With front + back or full wrap chosen, each item in Your design has Front and Back buttons, and the tumbler turns to show it. You can also turn it yourself with the buttons beneath it or by dragging.
  5. Send it to Jim. Add your name and email. Jim reviews every design and replies before anything is made.
